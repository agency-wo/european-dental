Cosmetic and functional rhinoplasty

Cosmetic and functional rhinoplasty

Regain the harmony of your profile and breathe freely again.

Rhinoplasty is one of the most requested and most delicate procedures: it is not just about “getting a nose job”, but about redefining the balance of the face while respecting who you are.

We offer two complementary approaches:

  • Cosmetic rhinoplasty, to improve the shape, proportion and harmony of the nose in relation to the face.
  • Functional rhinoplasty, to correct any breathing difficulties caused by internal deviations or malformations.

Every procedure is planned with a 3D digital simulation, which lets you preview the possible result and choose the most suitable solution together with the surgeon.

Recovery is quick and monitored: most patients return to normal life within 7-10 days.

Septoplasty

Septoplasty (correcting a deviated nasal septum)

Breathing well is the first step to living better.

Septoplasty is a procedure that restores proper breathing by correcting a deviated nasal septum.

It is often combined with functional rhinoplasty to improve both breathing and the appearance of the nose in a single procedure.

Our surgeons use minimally invasive techniques that reduce swelling and healing time, guaranteeing a stable, natural result.

The procedure is carried out under local or general anaesthesia, depending on the patient’s needs, and the hospital stay is usually 24 hours.

Otoplasty

Otoplasty (correcting protruding or asymmetric ears)

Bring harmony to your face with a gentle but decisive step.

Otoplasty corrects protruding, asymmetric or irregularly shaped ears, naturally improving the symmetry of the face.

It is a quick, safe procedure with permanent results, suitable for both men and women, including young people.

Thanks to new cartilage shaping techniques, scars are practically invisible and you can return to daily life after a few days.

Many of our patients describe this treatment as “a small change that transformed their self-confidence”.
