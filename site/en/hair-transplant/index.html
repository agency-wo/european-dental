Advanced techniques: FUE and DHI

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)

Today, the FUE technique is the most advanced standard in hair transplantation.

It is based on extracting individual follicular units (groups of 1-4 hairs) from the donor area, usually at the back of the head, and grafting them precisely into thinning or bald areas.

Main advantages:

  • No visible scarring
  • Quick recovery (3-5 days)
  • A natural, even result
  • Suitable for men and women

Every procedure is planned with digital simulation and dermoscopic analysis, to set the perfect density, direction and implant angle, creating a result that is aesthetically indistinguishable from natural hair.

DHI (Direct Hair Implantation)

DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) is the most modern evolution of FUE hair transplantation.

After extraction, the follicles are implanted directly, without first creating channels or incisions, using a special instrument called the Choi Implanter Pen.

DHI advantages:

  • Greater density per cm²
  • Full control over the implant angle
  • Even faster recovery
  • Minimal trauma to the skin
  • No full shave needed

It is the ideal solution for anyone who wants a natural, high-precision result with a shorter recovery time.

Complementary treatments and post-operative care

We take care of you before, during and after the procedure, with a medical and human approach that makes all the difference.

Included in our complete packages:

  • Analysis and personal consultation

  • PRP (platelet-rich plasma) treatment to stimulate regrowth

The result is natural, gradual regrowth, with strong, healthy and permanent hair.

Who is it for?

A hair transplant is suitable for:

  • Men and women with androgenetic alopecia (hereditary baldness)
  • Localised or general thinning
  • Post-surgical or traumatic scars
  • Patients who have already had a transplant and want to improve the result

Every case is unique: our team draws up a tailored treatment plan based on trichological analysis, lifestyle and aesthetic expectations.
