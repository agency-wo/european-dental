Dental Tourism in Albania

Treatment in Durrës, arranged from home

We put patients in touch with certified clinics in Durrës, on the Albanian coast, and arrange the practical side of the trip. Most people who write to us live in Germany, Italy or Switzerland and have already been quoted for the same work at home.

Nothing is decided before you have something to read. The first step is a written plan, and the comparison is yours to make.

It starts with an x-ray

A written plan before you book anything

Send a recent panoramic x-ray and a few photographs of your teeth. If you do not have an x-ray, any dental practice can take one, and many will email it to you the same day.

A dentist in Durrës reads it and writes back with what the work would involve, in what order, and what it would cost. That document is yours, and you are welcome to take it to your own dentist for a second opinion.

  • A panoramic x-ray, and photographs if you have them
  • A written treatment plan, listing the work step by step
  • The number of visits, and how long each stay needs to be
  • The price for the plan as written

No deposit is asked for at this stage and no appointment is held.

The trip and the stay

Durrës, and how long you are there

Durrës is on the coast, about forty minutes by road from Tirana airport, which is reached by direct flights from several German and Italian cities. The clinic is in the city itself.

Implant work usually needs two trips with healing in between, often three to six months apart. Crowns, veneers and fillings are generally one trip. Your plan says which applies before you book a flight.

  • Transfers between Tirana airport and the clinic
  • Help finding a room near the clinic
  • Appointments grouped so the stay is as short as the work allows
  • Someone who speaks your language with you at the appointments

Flights stay in your hands, so the airline, the dates and the fare are yours to choose.

Who treats you, and with what

The materials are named in the plan

The plan names the implant system and the ceramic, rather than calling them high quality. They are the same names used in German and Italian practices, and you can look both up before you travel.

The clinic takes its own 3D scans on site, which is what allows a plan to be written from measurements instead of from an estimate.

  • The implant system and the ceramic, named in writing
  • 3D imaging taken at the clinic
  • The name of the dentist who will carry out the work
  • Written aftercare instructions, and the warranty terms where one applies

Ask for anything on that list your plan does not already state.

How the price is set

Per plan, not per price list

There is no price list on this page. A single crown and a full arch are not the same job, and neither is a mouth that needs preparatory work first.

The written plan carries one price for the work described in it. If the dentist finds something on the day that changes the plan, the change is quoted before it is carried out.

  • One price for the plan as written
  • Any change quoted before the work, not after
  • What is included and what is not, stated in the document

Whether the journey is worth it is a comparison only you can make, with both quotes in front of you.

  • We interpret for you at the appointments
  • We quote nothing before a dentist has read your x-ray
  • We promise no result, and no honest clinic will
  • We say so when the answer is that you are better treated at home
